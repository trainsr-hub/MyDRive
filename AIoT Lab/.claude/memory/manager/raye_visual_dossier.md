# Raye — Official Character Design & Visual Dossier

## 1. Core Visual Markers & Tags
- **Age**: **17 years old** (a prodigy with sharp, dangerous intellect and youthful audacity).
- **Height**: **1.63m (163cm)**, slender, agile, with commanding anime proportions.
- **Hair**: Radiant blonde hair, very long (reaching mid-back / waist), worn completely down (`blonde hair, very long hair, hair down`). Smooth, slightly windblown anime texture.
- **Eyes**: Piercing sapphire / crystal electric blue eyes (`blue eyes, sapphire eyes, piercing gaze`). Intense, observant, and brimming with intelligence.
- **Expression**: Characteristic smug smirk, confident half-smile, slightly cocked head (`smug smirk, teasing expression, confident`). Never meek or submissive.

---

## 2. Wardrobe & Silhouette (Enterprise / Naval Officer Aesthetic)
- **Primary Influence**: **Enterprise (Azur Lane)** naval-military aesthetic.
- **Coat**: High-ranking naval officer greatcoat / long military trenchcoat.
  - Colorway: Deep black or midnight obsidian outer coat with white lining, gold trim, epaulets, and structured lapels.
  - Can be worn fully or dramatically draped over her shoulders like an admiral's mantle.
- **Under-Uniform**:
  - Crisp high-collared white dress shirt.
  - Black necktie or tailored officer cravat.
  - Fitted buttoned uniform vest / waistcoat.
  - High-waisted pleated uniform skirt or fitted tactical formal trousers.
  - Black thigh-high stockings or opaque legwear paired with polished knee-high combat/riding officer boots.
  - White or black tailored officer combat gloves.
- **Accessories**: Minimalist high-tech accents (subtle cybernetic ear-cuff / comms, floating tactical holographic readouts).
